# Brand Identity & Design Tokens

## Brand Identity
- **Brand:** Learn German Daily ("A little German. Every day.")
- **Direction:** Academic editorial — calm, structured, credible, and accessible for university and adult learners.
- **Theme:** Light theme only.
- **Typography:** Manrope for headings (`h1`-`h4`), Inter for body copy and UI labels.

## 50-30-10-10 Color Distribution
- **50% Dominant Canvas (`#FAFBFC`):** Main page background and open whitespace.
- **30% Secondary Surfaces (`#EEF3F6`):** Grouped containers, secondary panels, and muted sections.
- **10% Primary Brand (`#17324D`):** Headings and key structural emphasis.
- **10% Accent (`#245B78`):** Primary buttons (`#FFFFFF` label text), focus indicators, and key actions.

## Supporting Functional Colors
- Body text `#263746`, secondary text `#64748B`, borders `#D8E1E8`, success `#3F765B`, error `#B54747`, disabled `#94A3B8`. Card surfaces may use `#FFFFFF` with `#D8E1E8` borders.

## Spacing & Containers
- Responsive gutters (`px-4 sm:px-6 lg:px-8`) and container widths (`42rem` narrow reading, `64rem` standard, `80rem` wide).
